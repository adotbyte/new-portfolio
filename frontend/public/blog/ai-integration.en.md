# How I Integrated Claude AI Into My Portfolio

After buying a Raspberry Pi 5, I realized I needed my own portfolio. My passion for computers and the IT world has always been strong, so I really wanted to go public and have my own website online. I wanted future employers to see my real skills, not just some static "Word" file.

That's why I decided to build an AI agent into my portfolio. At first, it only had a few simple features, but that felt too limited to me. I kept exploring, digging deeper into how AI works, and gradually expanded it.

## How did it all begin?

The idea was simple: I wanted a visitor to be able to just ask "What can you do?" or "What are your projects?", and have the AI answer in language they could understand. That would be much more interesting than just reading a list of attributes.

I started with a basic Claude AI model that could answer questions. But then I realized the AI could be far more powerful if I gave it "hands" – so-called **tool-use** capabilities. That's how "AdotByte AI" was born – an assistant that can not only talk, but also perform real actions.

## What can my AI agent do now?

Today, my AI agent is much more than a simple chatbot. It has the following tools:

- **`get_github_repos`**: The AI can directly access my GitHub account and show the latest projects. A visitor can ask "What has Audrius built recently?", and the AI will display real, live data.
- **`send_contact_email`**: If a visitor wants to get in touch, the AI can forward a message straight to my email. This means there's no need to look for a contact form – everything can be done during the conversation.
- **`get_availability`**: The AI can check whether I am currently open to new projects or job offers.

In addition, since the AI answers questions, it has access to my "knowledge base" – information about my experience, skills, and projects. This allows it to respond accurately and in context.

## What did I learn while working with AI?

When I started integrating AI into my portfolio, I realized one important thing: **AI is not magic**. It's a tool that requires a clear understanding of how it works and careful effort.

Today I understand how AI works, and I have the drive to build optimized systems with the help of AI. For me, AI is not just an "answer machine", but also **an essential tool for every IT enthusiast**. It allows you to push past boundaries that previously seemed insurmountable.

## What does this mean for others?

If you're an IT enthusiast and haven't tried AI yet, I highly recommend getting started. You don't need to build a complex agent with `tool-use` right away. Start with a simple question: "How could AI make my daily life easier?"

In my case, the answer was this: AI can make my portfolio alive and interactive rather than static. And it worked.

Today my AI agent is not just a part of my portfolio – it's proof of my experience, creativity, and technical skills. And this is only the beginning.

# How I Integrated Claude AI Into My Portfolio

After buying a Raspberry Pi 5, I realized that I needed my own portfolio. My passion for computers and the IT world has always been strong, so I really wanted to present myself publicly and have my own website online. I wanted future employers to see my real skills, rather than some static "Word" file.

That's why I came up with the idea of implementing an AI agent into my portfolio. At first, it only had a few simple features, but that felt too limited to me. I kept exploring, diving deeper into how AI works, and gradually expanded it.

## How did it all begin?

The idea was simple: I wanted a visitor to be able to simply ask "What can you do?" or "What are your projects?", and have the AI respond in a language they could understand. That would be far more interesting than just reading a list of attributes.

I started with a basic Claude AI model that could answer questions. But then I realized that AI could be much more powerful if I gave it "hands" – so-called **tool-use** capabilities. That's how "AdotByte AI" was born – an assistant that can not only talk but also perform real actions.

## What can my AI agent do now?

Today, my AI agent is much more than a simple chatbot. It has the following tools:

- **get_github_repos**: The AI can directly access my GitHub account and show the latest projects. A visitor can ask "What has Audrius built recently?", and the AI will show real, live data.
- **send_contact_email**: If a visitor wants to get in touch, the AI can deliver a message straight to my email. This means there's no need to look for a contact form – everything can be done during the conversation.
- **get_availability**: The AI can check whether I'm currently open to new projects or job offers.

In addition, since the AI answers questions, it has access to my "knowledge base" – information about my experience, skills, and projects. This allows it to respond accurately and contextually.

## What did I learn while working with AI?

When I started integrating AI into my portfolio, I realized one important thing: **AI is not magic**. It's a tool that requires a clear understanding of how it works and careful effort.

Today, I understand how AI works, and I have the desire to build optimized systems with the help of AI. For me, AI is not just an "answer machine," but also a **must-have tool for every IT enthusiast**. It allows you to push past boundaries that previously seemed insurmountable.

## What does this mean for others?

If you're an IT enthusiast and haven't tried AI yet, I highly recommend starting. You don't have to build a complex agent with **tool-use** right away. Start with a simple question: "How could AI make my daily life easier?"

In my case, the answer was this: AI can make my portfolio alive and interactive, rather than static. And it worked.

Today, my AI agent is not just a part of my portfolio – it's proof of my experience, creativity, and technical skills. And this is just the beginning.
